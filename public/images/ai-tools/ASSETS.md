# AI carousel brand assets

- **Canva Magic Studio:** `canva.svg` is the unchanged **Canva Icon logo.svg** from [Canva's official logo kit](https://www.canva.dev/assets/connect/Canva-logos.zip), linked from its [brand guidelines](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/brand-guidelines/). Magic Studio is represented by the Canva product icon.
- **HeyGen:** `heygen.svg` is the unchanged compact symbol from the [official HeyGen brand kit](https://www.heygen.com/brand-kit), [source SVG](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/pdhqcmb1/production/83db2519472125eff4a047b07de4d49eb4d5f880-132x132.svg). The SVG contains its own embedded image and has no external asset dependency.
